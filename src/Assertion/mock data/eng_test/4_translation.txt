1. Medical history
    Medication history
    - NSAIDs for knee pain (discontinued)
    - omeprazole (started)

2. History of present illness
The patient was admitted for right upper quadrant abdominal pain lasting several weeks.
The illness began several weeks before admission with right upper quadrant pain, abdominal bloating, and transient nausea.
The patient reported no vomiting. The patient also had a long history of chronic back pain localized to the left paraspinal region of the mid-back, sometimes radiating to the anterior abdomen.
The abdominal pain was mainly localized to the right upper quadrant, occurred mostly after meals, and was accompanied by bloating.
Associated symptoms included transient nausea without vomiting. The patient also reported the chronic back pain described above. The patient was admitted for further evaluation, diagnosis, and treatment.
    Reason for admission: right upper quadrant abdominal pain
    Symptom onset: several weeks
    Current symptoms
    - right upper quadrant abdominal pain
    - abdominal bloating
    - transient nausea
    - vomiting
    - dull back pain
    Symptom characteristics
    - Location: right upper quadrant abdominal pain; back pain localized to the left paraspinal mid-back region and sometimes radiating to the anterior abdomen
    - Timing: commonly occurs after meals (abdominal pain and bloating)
    - Associated symptoms: bloating, transient nausea, vomiting, and chronic back pain
    Events before admission
The patient underwent outpatient esophagogastroduodenoscopy, which showed gastritis.
NSAIDs were discontinued.
The patient was treated with omeprazole.
Magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography showed stones in the distal common bile duct.
Liver function tests showed elevated liver enzymes.
The patient then presented to the Emergency Department for further evaluation and treatment.

3. Hospital assessment
Laboratory tests: liver function tests showed elevated liver enzymes.
Imaging and diagnostic studies: esophagogastroduodenoscopy showed gastritis. Magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography showed stones in the distal common bile duct.
Procedure performed: endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP). During ERCP, two stones were successfully removed from the distal common bile duct (CBD).
- Magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography showed distal common bile duct stones.
